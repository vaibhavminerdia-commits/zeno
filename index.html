Questions

Before you book

How do I book an appointment?

Use the form above or message us on WhatsApp. We reply with a confirmed time.

Do you offer bridal trials?

Yes. Every bridal package includes a trial so you can try the look and adjust it.

Which facial is best for glowing skin?

It depends on your skin type. A hydra facial, organic facial or cleanup are the usual choices, and the skin consultation is free.

Do you do waxing for sensitive skin?

Yes. We offer rica, liposoluble and regular wax, so you can pick the gentlest option.

Do you serve both men and women?

Yes. We are a unisex salon with separate service menus for each.
